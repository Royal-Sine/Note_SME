How to use Shop Ledger

A simple workflow for daily shop operations.

1 · Log a saleGo to Inventory → choose a product, quantity, amount and transaction date. Stock decreases automatically.
2 · RestockChoose Restock and enter the quantity. Stock increases automatically.
3 · Add your teamIn Tasks, Owner → Team → enter a name → tap “+ Add”. The new person immediately appears in the assignment list.
4 · Assign workChoose the staff member and deadline, then tap “Assign task”. Only Owner can do this.
5 · Work as StaffStaff only see their own tasks and can mark them done or read.
6 · HandoverUnfinished tasks stay visible for the next shift.
